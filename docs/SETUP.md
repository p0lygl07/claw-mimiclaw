# CLAW: MimiClaw — Setup

## First-time setup

> Some details below are marked **[confirm]** — fill them in with the exact specifics for
> your build before publishing.

### 1. Power it
Plug the MimiClaw into a USB port with a **data-capable** cable.

### 2. First boot
Give it a few seconds to start. An LED indicates power/activity [confirm].

### 3. Interact
Open a serial terminal at **115200 baud** [confirm] on the device's COM port, or follow
the interaction method specific to your build.
